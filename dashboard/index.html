Evidence-Aware Clinical AI

Master's project · Computer Science

Can an AI tell when its evidence isn't enough, and decide what to do about it?

Early sepsis prediction on real ICU stays. The system predicts, orders a lab group, or escalates when it is not confident. Every number below comes from one frozen protocol evaluated once on held-out patients.

Findings

  1. A calibrated model trained to work with missing labs reaches early-warning utility , in the range of strong PhysioNet 2019 entries.
  2. Knowing when it is unsure works: escalating of hours captures of all sepsis hours.
  3. Recorded labs add real value ( utility), but no deployable one-step lab-ordering policy recovers it; even an oracle gains only .

02 · Method

A pipeline built to avoid fooling ourselves

Leakage-safe at every step, with all thresholds chosen on validation data and sealed before the held-out test.

1 · Predict

Mask-aware XGBoost (26 labs, randomly hidden during training) with Platt calibration per missingness state.

2 · Decide or escalate

Make a call only when confidence max(p, 1−p) ≥ ; otherwise escalate. Chosen on validation for ≤ 1% risk.

3 · Acquire

For escalated hours, reveal one clinical lab group that was really recorded: fixed order, random, a learned patient-specific selector, or an oracle ceiling.

Two operating points, both frozen on validation

Early-warning alarm when risk ≥ (labs hidden and policies) or ≥ (labs as recorded), each chosen to maximise validation utility. The confidence rule above governs predict vs escalate. They answer different questions and are reported separately.

Limitations

  • Retrospective: “ordering” a lab reveals a value that was really recorded; we cannot know results of tests that were never ordered.
  • One public dataset, two hospitals; random patient split, no external site.
  • Calibration drifts above ~20% predicted risk (few rows; over-prediction).
  • Lead time is measured from the first alarm in the stay, an optimistic upper bound.
  • One-step, one-group acquisition only; timing and multi-step policies are future work.
