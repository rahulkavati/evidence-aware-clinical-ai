## 2. Selective prediction with escalation

When the model is not confident it does not predict; the row is escalated (acquire a lab group, wait, or refer to a clinician). Retained predictions at this operating point are rule-out (negative) decisions, so escalation is how positives are surfaced.

| System | Coverage | Risk on retained | Escalated rows | Positive rows escalated | Septic patients escalated before onset | Non-septic patients escalated |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| All 26 labs hidden (baseline) | 90.8% | 1.05% | 9.2% | 47.5% | 64.1% | 18.9% |
| Labs as recorded (full information reference) | 89.7% | 0.93% | 10.3% | 53.9% | 76.1% | 22.6% |
| Acquire: fixed clinical-group order | 91.8% | 1.07% | 8.2% | 45.7% | 62.5% | 15.8% |
| Acquire: random eligible group | 92.1% | 1.11% | 7.9% | 43.7% | 63.0% | 16.4% |
| Acquire: patient-specific selector | 92.2% | 1.09% | 7.8% | 44.4% | 61.4% | 15.5% |
| Acquire: oracle ceiling (uses labels) | 93.5% | 1.02% | 6.5% | 47.3% | 61.1% | 11.3% |

Trivial reference: predicting negative everywhere has 100% coverage and 1.81% risk.
